Cover: all 17 details in a grid on white, the title below. 17 close details of an acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. A bulky standing form occupies the centre, assembled from large overlapping ovals in dusky blue, mauve, pale pink, deep red and grey green, all edged in black. Its upper section is a cluster of small loops in red, yellow, green and blue, topped by green and black vertical strokes that fan out across the top edge.
