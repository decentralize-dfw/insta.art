Cover: all 10 details in a grid on white, the title below. 10 close details of an acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. A large white and pale blue mass of overlapping rounded units fills the upper two thirds over a black support, crossed by orange and ochre streaks and short yellow-green dashes. Two pale discs sit near its top; a separate white ovoid marked with two orange crosses floats at the upper right corner.
