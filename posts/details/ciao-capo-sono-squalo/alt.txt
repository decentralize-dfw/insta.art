Cover: a grid of all 20 details with the title in the middle. 20 close details of an acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. Two upright figures stand side by side, each built from clusters of overlapping ovoid units in cornflower blue, pale pink, red and white, outlined in black. The left figure fills the left half; its head has an open mouth lined with white spikes, and its body of stacked rounded lumps rests on two thick legs ending in clawed feet.
