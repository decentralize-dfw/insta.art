16 close details of an acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. A knot of figures stands against a flat black ground, carrying a body down toward a grey stone ledge that runs along the bottom edge. The carried body is pink and bare, larger than any of its helpers; its white-curled head has sunk onto the carrier's shoulder, its legs fold toward the lower left, and one arm hangs until the red-tipped fingers touch the step.
