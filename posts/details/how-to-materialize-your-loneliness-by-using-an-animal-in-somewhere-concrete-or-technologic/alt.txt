12 close details of an acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. A large frontal head fills the left half of a horizontal canvas, drawn in ochre, orange and pink, with wide almond eyes under black brows, a yellow nose, blue and red lips and pink streaks down both cheeks. The right half holds a blue upright form topped by a dark disc with a yellow curved mark, its centre a black and green panel crossed by red and white bars.
