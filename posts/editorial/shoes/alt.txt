10 shoes from 6 acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: sept neuf (a white court shoe with a red line along the sole); cellular spleens (a red heel sunk into the car); s(t)lop (a sneaker); duality of the angelic summarization (a heel drawn in white lines); viperella (a pointed dark shoe with a red heel); cute but unemployed cartoon figure (a white stiletto); sept neuf (the other court shoe); s(t)lop (the other sneaker, pink laces); duality of the angelic summarization (the other heel in white lines); viperella (the other pointed shoe).
