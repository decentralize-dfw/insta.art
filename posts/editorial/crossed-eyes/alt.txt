Cover: a grid of all 6 details with the word "crossed eyes" in the middle. Six close details from six acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each with eyes drawn as crosses. Details, in order: guarding holy mountains from malice (blue guard, a cross in each eye); good people helping bad ones (that’s why they’re bad too) (small red figure, white crosses for eyes); 7 fam board game (stacked pale blue heads, crossed eyes); wings of abyss (pale figure hanging upside down); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (small figure of rings, crossed eyes); baby rights 101 (diamonds marked with an X).
