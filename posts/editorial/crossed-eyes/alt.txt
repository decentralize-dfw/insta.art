Cover: a grid of all 10 details with the word "crossed eyes" in the middle. 10 figures with eyes drawn as crosses, from 8 acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: guarding holy mountains from malice (three blue guards, a cross in each eye); good people helping bad ones (that’s why they’re bad too) (three small red figures with white crosses for eyes); wings of abyss (a pink figure hanging upside down); 7 fam board game (pale blue heads with crossed eyes); society screwing balance (a figure hanging from a red line); articulo attack on mandarin (three figures on the boat, X for eyes); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (a figure of rings hanging upside down); good people helping bad ones (that’s why they’re bad too) (a white oval marked with two orange crosses); 7 fam board game (the eared clump at the edge of the board); despite transgression (a white tufted figure with a stitched mouth).
