Six close details from six acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each showing a piece of furniture. Details, in order: victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (a chair drawn in white lines); Virgil on Virtual Cage (final_final-finalV9) (an outlined chair on a pink panel); caprocorn sister: war, plague, earthquake (a hand resting on the stool); sept neuf (the coiled mint base of the armchair); walls of perception (the bowl on the red octagonal table); guarding holy mountains from malice (a stand on the yellow floor).
