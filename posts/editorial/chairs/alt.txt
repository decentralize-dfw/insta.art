Nine close details from seven acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each showing a piece of furniture. Details, in order: guarding holy mountains from malice (a chair hanging upside down from the ceiling); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (a chair drawn in white lines); sept neuf (the coiled mint base of the armchair); Virgil on Virtual Cage (final_final-finalV9) (an outlined chair on a pink panel); caprocorn sister: war, plague, earthquake (the dark green chest, its edges combed in lines); the assassination of the crow (the blue divan); sept neuf (the side table); walls of perception (the red octagonal table); guarding holy mountains from malice (the lamp).
