Six chairs and stands from acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: guarding holy mountains from malice (the chair hanging upside down from the ceiling); articulo attack on mandarin (a chair hanging upside down); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (the chair drawn in white lines); Virgil on Virtual Cage (final_final-finalV9) (the outlined chair on the pink panel); sept neuf (the mint armchair); guarding holy mountains from malice (the stand).
