Thirteen pieces of furniture from eight acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: guarding holy mountains from malice (the chair hanging upside down from the ceiling); articulo attack on mandarin (a chair hanging upside down); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (the chair drawn in white lines); Virgil on Virtual Cage (final_final-finalV9) (the outlined chair on the pink panel); sept neuf (the mint armchair); caprocorn sister: war, plague, earthquake (the dark green chest); the assassination of the crow (the blue divan); victim psycho-logique II : the peachy pharaoh connects with the chair (the red chair); sept neuf (the side table); caprocorn sister: war, plague, earthquake (the stool); walls of perception (the red octagonal table); guarding holy mountains from malice (the hanging lamp); guarding holy mountains from malice (the stand).
