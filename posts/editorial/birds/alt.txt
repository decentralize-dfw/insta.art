Nine close details from four acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each a bird. Details, in order: sept neuf (the pale bird on the side table); wings of abyss (the first beak); the assassination of the crow (the crow's head, a yellow eye); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird of the flock); wings of abyss (the second beak); the assassination of the crow (the crow, bent); articulo attack on mandarin (wings swept down); wings of abyss (the third beak); articulo attack on mandarin (another, wings back).
