Eight birds from five acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: sept neuf (the pale bird on the side table); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird of the flock, wings swept back); the assassination of the crow (the crow); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird diving, beak down); wings of abyss (one blue body, three beaks); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird crossing the flock); s(t)lop (the bird form, beak to the right); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird folding its wings).
