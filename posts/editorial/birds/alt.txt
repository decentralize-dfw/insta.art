Cover: all 13 details in a grid on white, the word "birds" below. 13 birds from 9 acrylic paintings by Yiğit Özen, each shown whole. In order: sept neuf (the pale bird on the side table); cute but unemployed cartoon figure (the creature, beak full of teeth); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird of the flock, wings swept back); the assassination of the crow (the crow); the family dinner after a night in jaïl (the middle head, a bird's beak and neck); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird diving, beak down); remixed execution of lucid drowning (the pink bird); wings of abyss (one blue body, three beaks); duality of the angelic summarization (the pale furred bird); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird crossing the flock); s(t)lop (the bird form, beak to the right); remixed execution of lucid drowning (the dark bird); articulo attack on mandarin (a bird folding its wings).
