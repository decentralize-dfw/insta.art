Acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. Filling the lower centre, a symmetrical orange figure built from ropy striated lines raises both arms outward in a wide loop; its head carries a green oval face with two lighter eye shapes and a yellow rayed crown, and concentric rings mark the chest and abdomen. Behind it a deep blue rectangle holds a gold rosette with radiating gold rays and a green leafed stalk descending from it.
