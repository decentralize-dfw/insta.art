Acrylic painting by Yiğit Özen. Two figures face each other across a chequered board turned corner on, its near corner meeting just above the bottom edge. At left a large head fills the upper corner, built from overlapping rounded lobes in rose, coral, mauve and ochre with no eyes and no mouth, one white and one yellow ring drawn on it and a pale tapering tube hanging from its underside; below, a dark aubergine jacket with a wide pale lapel line and a long gold wedge down its lower left runs to the bottom edge.
