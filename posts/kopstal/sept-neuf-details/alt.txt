Moving close-up details, ending with the complete work. Acrylic painting. A figure sits in a pale mint armchair in a green room, turned a little to the left, one hand raised with a cigarette in it. The skin is violet and mauve and the cropped hair is painted in dry white strokes. In place of one eye a white disc sits inside a black ring; the other eye is a pale drawn almond, and neither has a pupil. A sleeveless dress is given only by a thin yellow line laid over the body, which shows through it, and a cluster of dark red squares sits at the chest. The near hand rests on the chair arm with white nails hanging over its edge, and the legs cross and end in white court shoes with a red line along the sole. The base of the chair coils in translucent mint loops that the floor reads through. At right a pale bird with a yellow beak stands on the round green top of a black-outlined side table whose foot is built of flat panels, its head turned toward the figure. A dark green wall meets a bright green floor on a diagonal that rises from left to right. A small signature sits in the lower right corner.
