Sequence of genuine painting-stage photographs, ending with the finished work. Acrylic painting. Deep black fills most of the surface. Numerous elongated bird-like forms painted in blue and white, each with a long beak and streaked wings, scatter across the upper two thirds. Near the centre a thin blue-white humanoid figure grips a vertical pole. Along a horizontal grey boat in the lower third stand a row of small rounded figures built from stacked circles, each marked with an X for eyes. The word STUGOTZ appears in blue letters on the boat's side. At left rises a pale grey triangular peak. Two white anchors and a chain sit at lower left, and thin diagonal lines extend into dark blue water along the bottom.
